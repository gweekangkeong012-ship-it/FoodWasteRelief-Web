About Group 4 Initiative

We are a team of students at Asia Pacific University (APU) dedicated to addressing commercial food waste and student food insecurity within a 10-mile radius around our campus.

UN SDG 2: Zero Hunger

By deploying a real-time tracking network, our initiative connects surplus food from campus cafeterias directly to local orphanages and old folks' homes to relieve hunger and support community well-being.

UN SDG 12: Responsible Consumption

We aim to physically divert edible food surplus from ending up in landfills while educating the student body on sustainable consumption and food waste reduction habits.
